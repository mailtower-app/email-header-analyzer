# 📧 Email Header Analyzer

**Analyze email headers directly in your browser — privately, securely, and for free.**

The **Email Header Analyzer** helps you understand where an email came from and how it traveled across the internet. Analyze raw email headers, visualize the mail server path, and inspect technical details — **without sending your email data to a server**.

👉 **[Try the Email Header Analyzer](https://mailheader.mailtower.app)**

## 🔒 Privacy First

Your email headers can contain sensitive information. That's why the Email Header Analyzer is designed to process everything **locally in your browser**.

* 🔐 **100% client-side processing**
* 🚫 **No email headers are uploaded**
* 🌐 **No server-side analysis**
* 🛡️ **Your data stays in your browser**

You can analyze sensitive email headers without handing them over to a third-party service.

## ✨ Features

### 🛣️ Server Trace

Visualize the path an email took through the different mail servers.

Identify:

* Mail servers involved in delivery
* The route your email took
* Delays between mail servers
* Origin and destination information

### 🔍 Header Analysis

Inspect the technical information contained in email headers and better understand how an email was processed and delivered.

### 🔐 Privacy-Focused

All analysis is performed locally in your browser. The email header content itself does not need to leave your device.
